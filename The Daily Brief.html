Global Action Needed as Climate Change Impacts Rising

Scientists urge faster shift to renewable energy to protect coastal cities and agriculture.

By Hafsa | Published on November 22, 2026

Understanding the Current Situation

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. Recent reports show that average global temperatures have risen steadily over the past century due to human activities such as burning fossil fuels.

Extreme weather events, including heatwaves, heavy rainfall, and prolonged droughts, are becoming more frequent across many regions.
